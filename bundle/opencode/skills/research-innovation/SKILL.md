# Research & Innovation Skill

## Overview
Research design, technology scouting, and innovation management.

## Process
`
Research → Experiment → Prototype → Validate → Scale
`

## Agents
| Phase | Agents |
|-------|--------|
| Research | research-scientist, technology-scout, innovation-lead |
| Analysis | industry-analyst, domain-expert, market-researcher |
| Validation | model-evaluator, test-architect |

## When to Use
- Designing research experiments
- Evaluating new technologies
- Building proof of concepts
- Scouting vendor landscapes
- Planning R&D portfolios
