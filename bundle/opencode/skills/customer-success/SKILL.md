# Customer Success Skill

## Overview
Customer support, onboarding, retention, and training.

## Customer Journey
`
Onboard → Engage → Support → Retention → Expansion
`

## Agents
| Phase | Agents |
|-------|--------|
| Onboarding | customer-success, training-specialist |
| Support | support-engineer |
| Retention | customer-success, business-strategist |
| Expansion | partnership-manager, financial-analyst |

## When to Use
- Designing onboarding flows
- Building support operations
- Creating training programs
- Improving customer retention
- Planning expansion strategies
